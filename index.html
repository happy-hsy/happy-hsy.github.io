Haosen Yang 杨浩森

I am a Ph.D. student at the Centre for Vision, Speech, and Signal Processing (CVSSP) at the University of Surrey, supervised by Dr. Xiatian Zhu.

I am also a Visiting Student at Imperial College London, hosted by Dr. Jiankang Deng.

My current research focuses on unlocking the rich intelligence contained in video data to advance video MLLMs, video generation, World Action Models (WAMs), and Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs). Ultimately, I aim to enable richer interactions between humans and environments, humans and agents, and agents and environments.

Open to collaborate

I am always open to collaborations across spatial intelligence, video understanding and generation, and embodied AI. I am particularly interested in connecting video intelligence with real-world perception and action, and welcome collaborations involving robotic platforms or robot arms.
